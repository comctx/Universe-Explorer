Hidden Gems

Five Saturn facts that are hard to forget. Tap the hotspots to connect the facts to the interactive explorer.

DENSITY Saturn could float

Saturn is the only planet with an average density lower than liquid water. A hypothetical ocean large enough could make the whole planet float.

ATMOSPHERE 1,800+ km/h winds

Equatorial winds in Saturn's upper atmosphere can reach about 500 m/s — roughly 1,800 km/h.

NORTH POLE A giant hexagon

A persistent six-sided jet-stream pattern surrounds Saturn's north pole, with a powerful rotating storm near its center.

TITAN A moon with a dense atmosphere

Titan is the only moon in the Solar System with a dense atmosphere and has methane-and-ethane lakes, seas, clouds, and rain.

ENCELADUS Water sprayed into space

Jets of water vapor and icy particles erupt from cracks near Enceladus's south pole and feed material into Saturn's environment.

Exploration Missions

From the first close flyby to Cassini's 13-year orbital study.

1979Pioneer 11

First spacecraft to fly past Saturn and examine the rings up close.

1980Voyager 1

Detailed Saturn and Titan observations transformed our view of the system.

1981Voyager 2

Added new ring, moon, atmosphere, and magnetosphere measurements.

2004–2017Cassini–Huygens

Orbited Saturn for 13 years; Huygens landed on Titan in 2005.
