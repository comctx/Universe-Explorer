Understand Saturn

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second-largest planet. Its bright rings are made of countless pieces of ice and rock, from tiny grains to much larger chunks.

The Rings

Saturn's rings are huge in width but surprisingly thin compared with the planet.

Worlds Around Saturn

Some of Saturn's moons are destinations in their own right.

Titan

Saturn's largest moon has a thick nitrogen-rich atmosphere and lakes and seas of liquid methane and ethane on its surface.

Enceladus

This small icy moon sprays water-rich plumes from fractures near its south pole, evidence of a global subsurface ocean.

Iapetus

Iapetus is famous for its dramatic two-tone surface and a remarkable ridge that runs along much of its equator.

Cassini–Huygens

The mission that transformed our understanding of Saturn.

CASSINI
2004–2017

Cassini orbited Saturn for 13 years, studying the planet, rings, magnetic environment, and moons. The Huygens probe separated from Cassini and landed on Titan in 2005 — humanity's first landing in the outer solar system.
