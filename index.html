Known Since Antiquity

Mars is bright enough to see without a telescope. Ancient civilizations watched its reddish light and motion across the sky.

1610 · Galileo

Galileo Galilei made the first known telescopic observations of Mars.

1877 · Phobos & Deimos

Asaph Hall discovered Mars' two small moons.

Olympus Mons

The largest known volcano in the solar system is on Mars.

Valles Marineris

A giant canyon system stretches roughly 3,870 km (2,400 miles).

Water & Ancient Mars

Ancient valleys, deltas, lakebeds, and minerals show that liquid water once existed on the surface.

1965 · Mariner 4

The first successful Mars flyby returned the first close-up images of the planet.

1976 · Viking

Viking 1 and 2 became the first long-lived successful landers on Mars.

2021 · Perseverance & Ingenuity

Perseverance began exploring Jezero Crater, while Ingenuity achieved powered flight on another world.
