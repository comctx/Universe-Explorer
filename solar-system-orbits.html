Earth and Mars are shown in a simplified launch-window geometry. Mars begins about 44° ahead of Earth. The yellow Hohmann path curves around the Sun and meets Mars near the transfer intercept point.

What this display teaches

Inner planets orbit faster because they are closer to the Sun. Outer planets have much longer years. Mars also follows a slightly elliptical path rather than a perfect circle, which is visible here more clearly than Earth's nearly circular orbit. Turn on the Inner Planets view to spread Mercury through Mars apart, watch the asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter, and use the orbit trails to compare how quickly the planets sweep around the Sun.
